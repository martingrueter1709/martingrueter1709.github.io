Request accessHow it works

Upload once. Get back to the music.

Feed Stagecut your gig videos and it keeps your channels alive for as long as the footage lasts, so your head stays free for what matters: making and playing music.

1

You upload

Hand over your gig videos, plus a few reference photos so Stagecut can find you in the footage. That's your whole job.

2

Stagecut creates

It crops every clip to vertical and writes the title and caption in your voice, DJ-centric and never generic.

3

Stagecut posts

Two posts a day on each platform, at steady times, on its own. No logging in, no scheduling, no thinking about it.

Your accounts stay yours.

Nobody logs into your Instagram, TikTok or YouTube. You approve access once, and you can take it back at any time.

No passwords, no logins

You connect once through each platform's own official login screen. Your password never reaches us, and nobody ever signs in as you.

Official connections only

Stagecut uses the approved interfaces of YouTube, TikTok and Instagram. No workarounds, no bots, no scraping, so your accounts and your reach stay safe.

A human pace

About two posts per day per platform, with built-in daily limits that stay well within each platform's rules. No mass posting, no engagement tricks.

Only your content

Only your own footage, only to your own accounts. On Instagram, posts go out as Trial Reels, and making one public is always your call.

Private by default

Your reference photos never leave the computer running Stagecut. Nothing is sold or shared. Disconnect and delete your data whenever you want.

It sounds like you.

Stagecut crops it to vertical format and adds a title and caption in the creator's voice.

Before it writes a single word, Stagecut analyzes many of your previous captions, your word choice, tone, emoji habits and recurring lines, so every new title and caption is phrased the way you would phrase it. Not like a template.
